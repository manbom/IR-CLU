## n8n: An Open-Source Automation Tool

n8n is an open-source automation tool that lets people connect different tasks together without writing any code. With n8n, you can link apps and services to each other and automate your routine work.

Thanks to its impressive feature set, n8n even lets non-technical users design fairly complex processes simply by dragging and dropping.

Zapier, by contrast, is usually known as one of the more popular automation tools out there — but it comes with a few drawbacks that can push Iranian users toward n8n instead.

## The Advantages of n8n Over Zapier

1. **Free and open source**: One of n8n's biggest advantages is that it's open source. While Zapier charges significant fees for advanced features and certain integrations, n8n lets you use everything it offers at no extra cost.

2. **Self-hosting**: n8n lets you host the software on your own server, which matters a great deal for Iranian users. This means you can keep your data in a secure, reliable environment and sidestep issues around sanctions and access to foreign services.

3. **Support for a huge range of APIs**: Zapier supports more than 3,000 apps, but n8n's open-source nature means it can just as easily connect to custom and new APIs. That lets users meet their specific needs and optimize their processes by building whatever new connections they require.

4. **Simplicity and customization**: n8n lets users fine-tune their processes in more detail. For example, if you run an online store and want to move customer data from one source into another database, n8n lets you set that up simply and quickly.

## A Concrete Example

Say you run an online store and want every order automatically saved into an Excel file. With n8n, you can simply build a workflow that:
1. Calls your store's API to pull in all new orders.
2. Adds them to an Excel file.
3. Sends you an email confirming that the orders were saved correctly.

You can put this together in just a few clicks, without needing any deep technical knowledge.

## Conclusion

Thanks to its distinctive features, n8n can be the better option for Iranian businesses. Between the capabilities it offers for process automation and the ability to self-host, n8n looks well-suited to meeting your automation needs.
