## Why Tracking Competitor Prices Matters
In business, monitoring and analyzing your competitors' product prices is essential. It helps you understand your position in the market and adapt your strategy to changes as they happen. This is exactly where AI technology can play a key role — by automating the whole process.

## How Does an AI Agent Actually Work?
An AI agent built for tracking competitor prices can operate in several ways:
1. **Data collection**: using web scraping, the agent can visit your competitors' websites and extract prices and other relevant information. This can run daily, at set times.
2. **Data analysis**: once the data is collected, the agent can analyze it — looking at price changes, special offers, and product types.
3. **Reporting**: finally, the agent can hand you a comprehensive report on the state of the market, including a comparison of your competitors' prices and the analysis you need to make good decisions.

### A Concrete Example
Say you run an online cosmetics store and have several competitors in the same space. Using an AI agent:
- **It collects the data**: the agent visits your competitors' websites every day and extracts prices for comparable products.
- **It analyzes it**: you might discover that a competitor has suddenly dropped their prices. That information lets you respond — by lowering your own prices or running a special offer to win customers over.
- **It sends you a report**: the agent puts together a report covering price changes, best-selling products, and other analysis, so you can make better decisions.
