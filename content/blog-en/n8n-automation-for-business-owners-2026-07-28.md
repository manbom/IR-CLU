## What Is n8n, and Why Should You Care?

n8n is an open-source automation platform that lets people build and manage automated processes without any programming knowledge. It connects your different tools and pieces of software together, freeing up more of your time and energy to focus on the core of your business.

For example, say you run an online store and get hundreds of emails from customers or new inquiries every day. Instead of spending your time answering them one by one, you can set up automated workflows in n8n that reply to emails automatically or add customers straight into your CRM.

## How n8n Works

n8n works by connecting to different tools and services. For instance, you can connect n8n to services like Google Sheets, Slack, or Mailchimp. Once connected, you build a "workflow" that defines when and how data should move between these tools.

### A Concrete Example

Say you're a nutrition consultant who provides online meal plans to clients.

With n8n, you could set up a workflow that, whenever a new client signs up on your website:
1. Adds their information to a list in Google Sheets.
2. Sends them a welcome email.
3. Logs their information in your CRM so it's easier to manage going forward.

That way, instead of spending time doing all of this by hand, you can focus on delivering better service and higher-quality consulting.

## The Benefits of n8n

n8n is open source, which means you can use all of its features without paying anything for them. It's also highly customizable, so you can easily tailor your workflows to your business's specific needs.

On top of that, n8n makes it easy to monitor and manage your operations. Whenever something goes wrong or you need to change a workflow, editing it and keeping an eye on activity is simple and intuitive.
