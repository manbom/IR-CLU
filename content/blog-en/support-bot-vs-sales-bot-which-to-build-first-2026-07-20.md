## Introduction
Chatbots have become an essential tool for businesses today. They typically operate in two main areas: support and sales. Depending on your business's needs, deciding which type of bot to build first can have a big impact on your success.

## What Is a Support Bot?
A support bot typically answers customers' frequently asked questions and resolves their basic issues. These bots can:
- Provide general information to customers
- Answer frequently asked questions
- Identify common problems and offer appropriate solutions

For example, if you run an online store, a support bot can help customers with questions about the checkout process, delivery times, and return policies. This frees up your time for more important matters and automatically raises the level of your customer service.

## What Is a Sales Bot?
A sales bot is specifically designed to guide customers toward a purchase. These bots typically use marketing techniques and can:
- Present special offers to customers
- Notify them about discounts
- Help them choose products and make the buying process easier

Suppose you run a clothing store. A sales bot can persuade customers to buy by offering recommendations based on their taste and purchase history. This not only increases sales but also improves the overall shopping experience for users.

## Which Should You Build First?
Deciding which bot to build first depends on a few factors:
1. **Your business's current needs**: If your customers are currently facing a lot of questions and need help, building a support bot may be the higher priority. On the other hand, if you're looking for a quick boost in sales, a sales bot would be the better option.
2. **Speed and scale**: Support bots typically increase service efficiency and can help you manage high volumes of traffic. Sales bots, meanwhile, focus more on driving purchases and can have a bigger impact at specific moments.
3. **How they work together**: In some cases, one bot can support the other. For example, a support bot can gather the information a sales bot needs to make better offers to customers.

## Conclusion
Ultimately, the choice between a support bot and a sales bot depends on your business's specific needs. Both types of bots can play an important role in improving the customer experience and increasing sales. But understanding your own needs and priorities will help you make the best decision.
