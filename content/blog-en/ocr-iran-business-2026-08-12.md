## Introduction

Automation and AI-driven technologies have become an essential need for businesses today. One technology that's proving especially useful is Optical Character Recognition, or OCR — a technology that can automatically extract information from printed documents or images. This is particularly valuable for Iranian businesses, which often deal with large volumes of paperwork.

## Why OCR Matters for Iranian Businesses

Iranian businesses commonly face a range of challenges: piles of paper documents, difficulty searching for information, and time lost to manual processes. OCR technology makes it easy to solve these problems.

For example, a large goods-distribution company could use OCR to quickly extract information from invoices, waybills, and contracts. That data then becomes digital and searchable, cutting down on the inefficiency of hunting for information and the human error that comes with manual data entry — while saving a significant amount of time.

## How Does OCR Actually Work?

OCR works by converting images of text into data a computer can edit and process. The process involves the following steps:

1. **Image capture**: An image of the document or text is captured first.
2. **Preprocessing**: The image is cleaned up to improve quality and remove noise that could affect accuracy.
3. **Pattern recognition**: The OCR software identifies the letters and words in the image and converts them into text.
4. **Review and correction**: The extracted information is checked at this stage, and any errors are corrected.
5. **Final output**: The data is delivered in a usable format, such as text files or a database.

## The Benefits of Using OCR in Business

- **Time savings**: Automating the process means less time spent searching for and editing information.
- **Fewer human errors**: Automated processing improves accuracy and reduces the errors that come with manual entry.
- **Easier access to information**: Converting paper documents into digital form makes information easy to search and access.
- **Lower costs**: Spending less time on these processes helps cut operational costs.

## Conclusion

OCR technology can be a powerful tool for Iranian businesses, helping them navigate digital transformation. By cutting down on the problems that come with paper-based processing and improving accuracy, businesses can significantly boost their productivity.
